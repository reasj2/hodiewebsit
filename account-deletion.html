Deleting Your Account & Data

Last Updated: 2026-06-02

You can delete your Hodie account and personal data at any time. Here's how, and exactly what happens.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Hodie.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are removed right away.

Can't access the app?

Email support@migla.io from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll verify it's you and complete the deletion, normally within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, age, bio, gender, preferences)
  • All of your photos
  • Your matches and message history
  • Your swipes, likes, and interests
  • Your push token and device data

What we may keep, and why

To meet legal obligations and prevent abuse, we may retain a limited amount of data for a short period after deletion:

  • Records of reports/blocks and serious safety incidents — up to 2 years, to keep repeat offenders out.
  • Purchase/transaction records — as required by tax and accounting law (handled with Apple).
  • A minimal fraud-prevention record of the deleted email — briefly, then deleted.

These are kept only as long as the law requires or to protect our users, then permanently deleted. Backups are purged on a rolling 30-day schedule.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it separately on your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Hodie → Cancel.
