Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 2026-06-02

This Cookie Policy explains how SIA "Miglai" ("Hodie," "we," "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website hodieapp.one. For how the mobile app handles data and on-device storage, see our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. They let a site work properly and remember basic preferences. "Similar technologies" include local storage and pixels.

2. Cookies we use on hodieapp.one

Our website is primarily an informational and support site. We keep cookie use to a minimum:

TypePurposeRequired?
Strictly necessaryMake the site load, keep it secure, and remember your cookie choice.Yes — always on
Analytics (if enabled)Aggregate, privacy-respecting statistics about page visits, so we can improve them. Set only with your consent.No — optional

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on our website.

3. Your choices

  • Where we use non-essential (analytics) cookies, we ask for your consent and you can change your choice at any time.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site working.

4. Mobile app

The Hodie app does not use browser cookies. It uses on-device storage (Keychain, UserDefaults) and, for advertising, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only with your permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt. See the Privacy Policy, Sections 5 and 12.

5. Contact

Questions about cookies: support@migla.io
