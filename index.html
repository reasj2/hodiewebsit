Frequently asked

A few quick answers.

What makes Hodie different from other dating apps?

Hodie is built around one daily photo, captured live with both cameras. No camera-roll uploads. No recycled selfies. You see people as they actually are, today.

Is Hodie free?

Yes. The core features — daily photos, swiping, matching, and messaging — are free and have no ads. Optional Hodie+ and Hodie Gold subscriptions unlock advanced features like seeing who liked you, unlimited swipes, and Spotlights.

How do you keep the community safe?

Every account is phone-verified. Our moderation team reviews reports within 24 hours. You can block or report any user with one tap. Read more in our Community Guidelines.

How is my privacy protected?

We don't sell your data, don't run ads, and don't track you across other apps. Servers are in the EU. You can export or delete your data anytime in Settings. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
