Safety Center

Last updated: 2026-06-10

Hodie is built so that what you see is real — one live, unedited photo a day. But real safety is bigger than real photos. Here's how to protect yourself, and how we help.

If you're in danger right now

Contact your local emergency services — in the EU dial 112, in the US dial 911. Do that first; everything on this page comes second.

How Hodie protects you by design

  • Live photos only. Profiles are a same-day, dual-camera capture — no uploads, no filters, no years-old gallery. What you see is who shows up.
  • Match-first messaging. Only mutual matches can talk. Strangers can't contact you.
  • Report & block everywhere. Every profile and conversation has a report and block button. Reports are anonymous and human-reviewed. How reporting works.
  • 18+ only. Hodie is for adults. Accounts suspected of being underage are removed.
  • Pause anytime. Hide your profile without deleting anything via Settings → Pause profile.

Chatting safely

  • Keep conversations inside Hodie at first — scammers push to move you to other apps quickly.
  • Never send money, gift cards, or crypto to someone you met on a dating app. No exceptions, no matter the story.
  • Don't share your home address, workplace, daily routine, or financial details with someone you haven't met.
  • Be wary of anyone who can never video-call or meet — and of love-bombing that escalates unusually fast.
  • Trust your gut. If something feels off, it probably is — unmatch, block, report.

Meeting in person

  • Meet in a public place — a café, a bar, somewhere with people around. Not your home, not theirs.
  • Tell a friend who you're meeting, where, and when. Share your live location if you can.
  • Arrange your own transport there and back, and keep your phone charged.
  • Keep an eye on your drink, and drink within your limits.
  • It's always okay to leave early. You owe no one an explanation.

Intimate images and consent

  • Always get clear consent before crossing into intimate or sexual topics — and respect a no instantly.
  • Never share intimate images of someone without their consent. It's banned on Hodie and illegal in much of the world.
  • If someone threatens to share your images ("sextortion"), don't pay — stop contact, screenshot everything, report them, and contact the police. You are not in trouble; they are.

Crisis resources

  • Emergency (EU): 112  ·  Emergency (US): 911
  • EU victim support: 116 006 (free, many EU countries)
  • Latvia — Skalbes crisis line: +371 67222922 (24/7)
  • US — RAINN sexual assault hotline: 1-800-656-4673
  • Suicide & crisis (US): 988  ·  Worldwide directory: findahelpline.com

Our rules and your reports

What's allowed on Hodie is spelled out in the Community Guidelines — zero tolerance for harassment, hate, minors, or non-consensual content. When you report, a human reviews it, typically within 24–48 hours. For anything else, email support@migla.io.
