Support

The fastest way to reach a human is email. We answer in English and respond within a few business days. For urgent safety issues, please use the in-app Report button — those jump the queue.

Contact

For anything — general support, privacy, safety reports, appeals, or legal notices — email support@migla.io.

Please include the topic in your subject line so we can route your message quickly.

In-app actions

  • Report a user — tap … on their profile or chat → Report.
  • Block a user — tap … on their profile or chat → Block.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete Account.
  • Manage your subscription — open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Only Apple can cancel or refund.
